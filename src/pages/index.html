The Lancashire day

A county-sized grandstand

The peloton crosses into Lancashire on the Morecambe Bay coast, tackles its first big climb, the Côte de Jubilee Tower above Lancaster, then threads the fells of the Forest of Bowland and the market towns of the Ribble Valley before heading south towards Merseyside.
