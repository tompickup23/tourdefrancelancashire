The Lancashire day

A county-sized grandstand

Stage 2 falls on a Saturday, which turns a single afternoon of racing into a proper Lancashire weekend. The peloton crosses into the county on the Morecambe Bay coast, tackles its first big climb (the Côte de Jubilee Tower above Lancaster), then threads the wild fells of the Forest of Bowland and the market towns of the Ribble Valley before heading south towards Merseyside.
